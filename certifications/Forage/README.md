## Robotics and Controls Job Simulation: Johnson & Johnson MedTech

<img width="200" alt="Johnson & Johnson MedTech logo" src="jj-medtech-logo.png" />

* **Platform:** Forage | **Completed:** April 20, 2026
* [View Certificate (PDF)](./forage-jj-robotics-controls-certificate.pdf)
* [About this simulation](https://www.theforage.com/johnson-and-johnson/robotics-controls-aozc)

**What I did**

As a robotics & controls engineering intern in the simulation, I worked on optimizing a surgical robotic arm's performance:

* Used Python-based tools to diagnose control system inefficiencies, identify root causes of delays, and implement targeted optimizations.
* Proposed design modifications using annotated technical visuals, and checked their impact on responsiveness and durability through iterative testing.
* Developed a professional design proposal outlining findings, solutions, and recommendations for improving precision and reliability in robotic systems.

**What I took away**

This program confirmed that I really enjoy working on engineering solutions that improve precision and durability in robotic systems.

**Skills:** Python · Diagnostic analysis · Design optimization · Technical proposals
